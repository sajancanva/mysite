About

Teaching, research, curriculum design and educational innovation.

Dr. K. S. Sajan is a faculty member at the Research Centre in Education, NSS Training College, Ottapalam, Kerala. He holds an M.Sc. in Zoology, M.A. in Sociology, M.Ed. in Educational Technology, M.Phil. and a Ph.D. in Zoology (Insect Physiology and Biochemistry) from the University of Calicut. He has also earned certifications from several international universities through online learning platforms.

His published work includes state-level textbooks, handbooks and modules in Biology, Population Education and Home Science, along with research-based textbooks and over 30 research papers and articles.

He is an expert in Educational Research, Statistics and Educational Technology, and has organised and led conferences, workshops and training programmes in these areas for college teachers, school teachers and academicians.
